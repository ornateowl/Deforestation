The Solution

There are many ways for you to help alleviate the negative impacts of deforestation

Reduce consumption. Since a major cause of deforestation is agriculture, it is a good idea to buy only as much as you will eat and eat your leftovers later rather than wasting them. This will ensure that as many trees will not have to be cut down.

Buy secondhand furniture rather than buying new. It is not a good idea to support companies like IKEA which are tied to illegal logging practices. Instead of buying the cheap, mass-produced, and illegally-sourced furniture from IKEA and Walmart, you can buy secondhand and cut down fewer trees.

Go vegan. 75% of deforestation is driven by agriculture, with beef production being the cause of 41% of deforestation. Trees need to be cut down to plant food for animals to support meat and dairy farming, and more farm animals require more space.

Tell your friends! Change can't happen with just a few people. It is the cumulative impact that matters.
