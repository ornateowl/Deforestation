The Deforestation Issue

Have you ever wondered why deforestation is such a big issue? In reality, it is a problem for all humans, animals, and ecosystems. Trees act as carbon sinks and shield humans from deadly diseases and climate change. They also keep the soil in place and make it more fertile. Agriculture has become a problem and it is our duty to stop deforestation and protect ecosystems! Check out the "problem" and "solution" pages in the navigation bar above to learn more.
